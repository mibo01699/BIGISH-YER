## 🔍 Detailed Breakdown of Expenses

### 1. Web3 Engineering & Core Smart Contracts ($35,000)
- **Lead Smart Contract Architect (6 Months):** $18,000
  - Integration of the dual-wallet split engine (Pi GCV and YER Token mechanics).
- **Core Node Infrastructure & Testing Environments ($12,000):**
  - Maintenance of localized nodes, Testnet validation tools, and synchronization relays inside Yemen's infrastructure.
- **Independent Third-Party Cryptographic Audit ($5,000):**
  - External vulnerability scanning of the `YERTokenContract.js` file to assure international donors against exploits.

### 2. Localized Mobile Web Optimization ($20,000)
- **Frontend Engineer (UX/UI for Pi Browser Enclosure) [6 Months]:** $14,000
  - Structuring zero-refresh pages to work seamlessly under Yemen's internet disruptions and within the strict Pi browser wrapper.
- **Offline-First Synchronization Framework ($6,000):**
  - Building offline caching modules so producers can log batches (GAV Protocol) offline and sync securely when network blocks become active.

### 3. Field Deployment & Producer Onboarding ($25,000)
- **Field Coordinators & Youth Enumerators (Sanaa/Aden/Al-Mahwit) [10 Agents x $1,000]:** $10,000
  - Mobilizing youth trained via the **AJYAL platform** to onboard local farmers and micro-merchants onto the mobile ecosystem.
- **Merchant Incentive & Pilot Education Programs ($10,000):**
  - Conducting workshops on managing hybrid assets, securing wallets, and validating GCV pricing stability models.
- **Hardware Logistics & Secure Field Communications ($5,000):**
  - Securing entry-level smartphone devices for rural producer cooperatives to interact with the platform.

### 4. Legal Compliance & Anti-Fraud Monitoring ($12,000)
- **Institutional Compliance Counsel ($7,000):**
  - Ensuring the hybrid token model adheres strictly to anti-money laundering (AML) standards and international cross-border aid delivery regulations.
- **Pi KYC Interface Auditing & Data Privacy ($5,000):**
  - Hardening the internal database to ensure zero linkage of user biometric data outside the secure Pi authentication barrier.

## 🚫 Crucial Zero-Risk Guarantees for International Donors
1. **No Capital Extraction for Liquidity:** 100% of the requested USD funding is directed strictly to product building, engineering, and localized operational infrastructure. **Zero donor dollars** will be used to purchase or prop up the valuation of YER or Pi, eliminating financial exposure.
2. **Open-Source Digital Public Good (DPG):** All assets paid for by this funding are deployed under the **MIT License**, remaining permanently transparent, auditable, and globally accessible to eliminate corporate capture or administrative leakage.
