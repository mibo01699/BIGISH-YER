## ⚖️ 2. Macro-Pegging & Inflation Shield Mechanism

Unlike traditional fiat currencies, the digital **YER Token** is engineered with a continuous structural premium to protect local capital from hyperinflation.

### The Value Equation:
$$1 \text{ Digital YER} = 10 \text{ Physical Paper YER}$$

### Live USD Valuation Framework:
The digital wallet computes asset value dynamically relative to the physical paper currency's market rate against the United States Dollar (USD):

$$\text{Digital YER Value (USD)} = 10 \times \left( \frac{1}{\text{Market Exchange Rate of Paper YER against USD}} \right)$$

### Technical Advantage of the 1,000,000 Micro-units:
By dividing 1 YER into **1,000,000 distinct sub-units (micro-units)**, the ledger bypasses the physical limits of paper currency. Even during intense fiat depreciation, the smart contracts adjust fractional micro-units dynamically to settle micro-transactions (e.g., automated employment rewards, daily essential trades) with zero ledger friction.
