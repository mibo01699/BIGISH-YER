## 2. YER Token Supply & Official Distribution Matrix

The BIGISH-YER ecosystem defines a fixed maximum supply of:

# 300,000,000 YER

The supply is permanently divided into three strategic allocation
categories:

| Allocation | Percentage | Amount |
|---|---:|---:|
| Community & Public Utility Distribution | 10% | 30,000,000 YER |
| Ecosystem Launch & Liquidity | 30% | 90,000,000 YER |
| A.E.C Sovereign Fund Reserve | 60% | 180,000,000 YER |
| **TOTAL** | **100%** | **300,000,000 YER** |

### 2.1 Community & Public Utility Distribution — 10%

**Allocation: 30,000,000 YER**

Purpose:

- Pioneer/community utility incentives
- Verified ecosystem participation
- Merchant and service adoption
- Public utility programs
- Ecosystem growth incentives

This allocation is subject to eligibility, anti-abuse,
and governance controls.

### 2.2 Ecosystem Launch & Liquidity — 30%

**Allocation: 90,000,000 YER**

Purpose:

- Ecosystem token launch mechanisms
- Product utility
- Liquidity provisioning where officially supported
- Ecosystem application integration
- Approved market infrastructure

Pi DEX/AMM references in this repository are treated as
development/Testnet integrations unless and until the relevant
Mainnet functionality is officially available and authorized.

### 2.3 A.E.C Sovereign Fund Reserve — 60%

**Allocation: 180,000,000 YER**

Purpose:

- Sovereign financial programs
- Strategic ecosystem reserves
- Payroll and public-service programs
- Microfinance and productive financing
- Infrastructure and emergency programs

The reserve is subject to explicit governance,
vesting, authorization, and audit controls.

### 2.4 Supply Integrity

The three allocations MUST always satisfy:

30,000,000
+ 90,000,000
+ 180,000,000
= 300,000,000 YER

No allocation may cause the lifetime YER supply
to exceed 300,000,000 YER.
